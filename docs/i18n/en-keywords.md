## 2. Where the German angle doesn't carry over

| DE page | Problem | English recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Redeanteil (glossary + guide) | "Talk ratio" and "talk-to-listen ratio" both get used. The ranking pages (Gong, HubSpot, Sybill, Hyperbound, Crunchbase, Aircall) almost all have **"talk-to-listen ratio"** in the title. | **"Talk-to-listen ratio" wins** for the slug, H1 and title. Use "talk ratio" as a secondary term and in the app UI, and "talk time" in body text. |
| Redeanteil glossary | The DE text says "there is no fixed benchmark." In the US SERP, **43:57** (HubSpot/Gong, 25,537 calls, 2016) is the expected answer, and Gong's newer data (2025, 60:40 average) sits next to it. A page that leaves out the number won't be cited. | Name 43:57 with its source and year, put Gong 2025 next to it, then add the nuance (depends on call type: discovery vs. demo). This also suits GEO, because AI search engines pull numbers that come with a source. |
| Redeanteil guide | "Bedarfsanalyse" has no 1:1 equivalent. | Always "discovery call" / "discovery". The guide targets the how-to ("talk less"). The glossary targets definition plus benchmark. That keeps the two pages from competing for the same query. |
| Einwand "zu teuer" | The US SERP talks about "price objection", "compared to what?" and Feel-Felt-Found, and most results are long lists (HubSpot has 44 objections). | Use "price objection" as the main term. Keep "Compared to what?" (it fits the existing answer 1). Use a dollar example for the ROI math. Add one sentence on Feel-Felt-Found, because the SERP expects it. |
| Loqui vs. Cluely | In the US, Cluely is known as a stealth/interview tool ("undetectable"). The "Cluely alternative" SERP is full of interview-cheating tools. | Explicitly position Loqui as **"Cluely alternative for sales teams"** and don't use "undetectable" or "stealth" as keywords (brand risk). Show prices in USD: Cluely $19.99 / $149.99, Loqui $19. A tl;dv review mentions an ARR controversy at Cluely (March 2026). That's a single source, so **don't use it.** |
| Zoom (all integrations) | US-specific issue: **call-recording consent**. Several states (e.g., California) require all-party consent. People ask about this. | Add an FAQ: "Do I need consent to use an AI assistant on Zoom calls?" Answer it factually and with no legal advice, and point to the in-app consent confirmation. Use the English macOS labels: *Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording › System Audio Recording Only*. |
| Brand / entity | "Loqui" is also the name of a Mac dictation app, which is even more visible in the US. | Use one consistent entity name: **"Loqui, the real-time AI sales coach for Mac"**. Entity sentence (EN): *"Loqui is a Mac app that listens to your video calls and shows live answers from your own documents, the next question to ask, and your talk ratio. No bot joins the meeting."* Schema `priceCurrency` on `/en/` should be USD. |
| Comparison set | In the US, the "real-time sales coach" SERP is dominated by Revenue.io, Balto, Cirrus Insight (Live Meeting Coach), Dialpad and Gong. **Tenali AI** is a direct competitor: Mac app, no bot, answers from a knowledge base. | Add to the EN editorial plan: `loqui-vs-tenali`, and a fair "best real-time AI sales coaches" overview. Gong/Otter/Fireflies/Granola stay as planned. |
