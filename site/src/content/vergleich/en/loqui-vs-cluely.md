## What both tools do

Both run in their own window on your computer, listen to the call, and show you suggestions while you talk. Neither one joins the meeting as a bot. That sets both apart from note-takers like Granola or Fireflies, which only help once the call is over.

## The real difference: sourced answers and coaching

Loqui answers from your own docs: pricing, case studies, objection handling, competitive notes. Every answer shows its source. If it’s not in your docs, Loqui tells you so instead of making up a number. On a sales call, that’s the difference between help and a liability.

Cluely draws on general AI knowledge, and you can add your own instructions and files.

Then there’s the coaching. Loqui shows your talk ratio live and nudges you when you slip into a monologue. After the call, you get feedback with timestamps: what worked and what to do differently next time. You get better with every call instead of just getting through it.

## When Cluely is the better fit

- You need it on Windows or iPhone, not just Mac.
- On the call, you mostly need general expertise, like technical explanations, more than answers from your own docs.
- You need support for many languages. Cluely lists more than 12.

## When Loqui is the better fit

- You run sales calls, investor pitches, or team one-on-ones, and you want answers to come from your own docs.
- You want to get better at running calls: talk ratio, monologues, the question you forgot to ask.
- You want every answer to come with a source, so you never quote the wrong price or promise something you can’t deliver.
