## How to set up Loqui for Zoom

1. Open Loqui and allow **microphone** access when prompted.
2. In System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen & System Audio Recording, turn on **System Audio Recording Only** for Loqui.
3. Confirm that everyone on the call knows you’re using Loqui, then click **Start coaching**.
4. Join your Zoom call as usual.

From then on, your microphone is “Me” and the Zoom audio is “Customer.” Headphones give you the cleanest split, because sound from your speakers can’t leak back into the mic.

## What you see during the call

A slim bar sits at the top center of your screen with the time, your talk ratio, and the latest suggestion. When the prospect raises an objection or asks a direct question, an answer card opens below it with a line you can say right away and the source from your docs. Press Option-Space anytime to ask Loqui yourself: “What should I say?”
